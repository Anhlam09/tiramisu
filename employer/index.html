For employers

Tiramisu as an employee benefit

A look at what an HR team at Northwind Health, a fictional company of 2,400 employees, could see. Totals only, never anyone's story.

How hours are estimated: steps completed × 20 average minutes saved per step ÷ 60. That is 3,420 × 20 ÷ 60 = 1,140 hours. The per-step figure is an assumption, not a measurement.

Privacy principles

  • HR sees only totals, never one person's activity.
  • HR never sees names, estate details, documents, or money.
  • Groups smaller than 10 are hidden, so no one can be picked out.
  • Using Tiramisu is always the employee's own choice.

How it works for employees

  1. Step 1

    Employee signs up

    Through the benefits portal, with a personal login that the employer cannot see into.

  2. Step 2

    Family gets guidance

    A calm roadmap, checklists, and plain-English answers for the weeks after a loss.

  3. Step 3

    HR sees anonymous totals

    Only counts, like families supported and steps completed, once a group is large enough.
